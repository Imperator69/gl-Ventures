01 / Complex project delivery

Complex Services

Making ambitious projects work.

Complex Services combine project development, partner coordination and operational execution. GL Ventures structures demanding assignments and coordinates delivery across disciplines, organisations and borders.

Learn more: Complex Services
  1. 01

    Project development & management

    Turning an ambitious objective into a workable plan: workstreams, responsibilities, budgets and milestones. We then coordinate everyone involved and keep delivery on track.

  2. 02

    Partners & stakeholders

    Identifying suitable partners, structuring collaboration and coordinating the interests of businesses, investors and public institutions. Creating the conditions for clear decisions and lasting partnerships.

  3. 03

    International projects

    Supporting cross-border partnerships and business activities: from selecting markets and partners to coordinating practical delivery with local counterparts.

  4. 04

    Operational execution

    Taking responsibility for defined workstreams and coordinating external specialists. This includes project organisation, support for site development and permitting processes, and preparing well-founded management decisions.

02 / Strategic business advice

Strategic Advisory

Clarity for your next business decision.

Strategic Advisory creates a sound basis for decisions on growth, positioning and new business opportunities. GL Ventures combines market and business model perspectives with clear priorities, business cases and an actionable roadmap.

Learn more: Strategic Advisory
  1. 01

    Positioning & business strategy

    We sharpen the business model, strategic positioning and value proposition. Together, we define which opportunities to pursue and what needs to be in place to pursue them.

  2. 02

    Growth & market access

    We structure growth options, assess target markets and develop approaches to business development, partnerships and international collaboration.

  3. 03

    Business cases & decisions

    We turn strategic options into robust business cases. Assumptions, opportunities, risks and resource requirements become transparent and comparable for management decisions.

  4. 04

    Governance & roadmap

    We connect strategy with a clear way forward: priorities, responsibilities, decision processes and milestones. Turning direction into a practical implementation plan.

03 / Building new businesses

Venture Building

From a business idea to a viable company.

Venture Building connects business model development with building the company itself. GL Ventures supports the business idea, business case, partnerships, governance, funding preparation and operational execution.

Learn more: Venture Building
  1. 01

    Idea & market validation

    We assess target customers, market needs and commercial assumptions. The result is a clear business thesis and practical next steps for market validation.

  2. 02

    Business model & business case

    We structure the value proposition, revenue model, cost base and growth path. The business case provides a basis for entrepreneurial decisions and funding discussions.

  3. 03

    Partnerships & company development

    We develop the partnership and governance structure, prepare funding discussions and coordinate company development with the relevant specialists.

  4. 04

    Market entry & execution

    We turn the concept into an operational roadmap and support market entry, focusing on customer outreach, business development and the coordination of the next growth steps.

Questions & answers

GL Ventures at a glance.

01What does GL Ventures do?

GL Ventures provides Complex Services, Strategic Advisory and Venture Building. We support businesses, investors and public stakeholders with complex projects, strategic decisions and the development of new companies.

02How do the three services differ?

Complex Services focus on managing and delivering demanding projects. Strategic Advisory provides the basis for strategic decisions. Venture Building combines business model development with building a company. The services can be combined depending on the project.

03Who does GL Ventures work with?

Businesses, investors and public stakeholders. Typical clients are leadership teams and capital providers who need to structure a complex project, prepare a strategic decision or build a new company.

04Which sectors does GL Ventures cover?

Our areas of expertise are defense and dual use, energy and infrastructure, innovation and technology commercialisation, blockchain and tokenisation, and digitalisation.

05Does GL Ventures also handle execution?

Yes. We take responsibility for defined workstreams, coordinate external specialists and support project organisation, site development and permitting processes, and the preparation of management decisions.

06Where does GL Ventures work?

GL Ventures is based at Wollzeile 18/10, 1010 Vienna, Austria. We also support international and cross-border projects and work in German and English.

07How does an engagement start?

Contact Dominik Lerch or Gianluca Graf by email or phone. In an initial conversation, we discuss your objective, constraints and support needs, then define an appropriate scope and next steps.
