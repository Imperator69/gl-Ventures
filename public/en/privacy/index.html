Privacy policy

Controller

GL Ventures GmbH, Wollzeile 18/10, 1010 Vienna, Austria. Email: lerch@gl-ventures.at, graf@gl-ventures.at.

Principle

We process personal data only in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG). This website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools and loads no third-party content. Fonts and images are served directly from our own server.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by World4You Internet Services GmbH, Austria. When you visit the website, the server processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested page, amount of data transferred, referrer, and browser and operating system.

This processing serves the secure and stable operation of the website and is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted automatically in line with the hosting provider’s retention rules. A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is in place with the hosting provider.

Contact by email or phone

If you contact us, we process your details to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in responding (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep the data for as long as the enquiry requires or statutory retention obligations apply. We do not share it without your consent.

External links

This website contains links to LinkedIn. Data is only transferred to LinkedIn once you click such a link. LinkedIn’s privacy policy then applies.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Please contact us at one of the email addresses above.

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at).
