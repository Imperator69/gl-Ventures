02 / Strategic business advice

Strategic Advisory

Clarity for your next business decision.

Strategic Advisory creates a sound basis for decisions on growth, positioning and new business opportunities. GL Ventures combines market and business model perspectives with clear priorities, business cases and an actionable roadmap.

Get in touch

Typical situations

When we come in.

  • A new business area or market needs to be assessed before resources are committed.
  • Several growth options compete for budget and attention.
  • Positioning and value proposition have become blurred.
  • A decision needs a robust business case for management, boards or investors.

Service modules

What we take on.

  1. 01

    Positioning & business strategy

    We sharpen the business model, strategic positioning and value proposition. Together, we define which opportunities to pursue and what needs to be in place to pursue them.

  2. 02

    Growth & market access

    We structure growth options, assess target markets and develop approaches to business development, partnerships and international collaboration.

  3. 03

    Business cases & decisions

    We turn strategic options into robust business cases. Assumptions, opportunities, risks and resource requirements become transparent and comparable for management decisions.

  4. 04

    Governance & roadmap

    We connect strategy with a clear way forward: priorities, responsibilities, decision processes and milestones. Turning direction into a practical implementation plan.

Outcomes

What you walk away with.

Sharper positioning

Assessed options

Robust business cases

Transparent assumptions, opportunities, risks and resource requirements.

An actionable roadmap

Priorities, responsibilities, decision processes and milestones.

FAQ

Questions about Strategic Advisory

01What does Strategic Advisory at GL Ventures cover?

Positioning and business strategy, growth and market access, business cases as a basis for decisions, and governance and a roadmap for implementation.

02Does the work end with a strategy paper?

No. The result is an actionable roadmap with priorities and responsibilities. If required, we continue to support implementation as part of Complex Services.

03Which sectors is Strategic Advisory suited to?

We combine strategic advisory in particular with experience in defense, energy, innovation, blockchain and digitalisation.
