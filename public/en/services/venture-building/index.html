03 / Building new businesses

Venture Building

From a business idea to a viable company.

Venture Building connects business model development with building the company itself. GL Ventures supports the business idea, business case, partnerships, governance, funding preparation and operational execution.

Get in touch

Typical situations

When we come in.

  • A business idea looks promising, but the market and assumptions have not yet been tested.
  • A technology needs to be turned into a viable business model.
  • A new business is to be built as a standalone company with partners.
  • Funding discussions are coming up and need a robust business case.

Service modules

What we take on.

  1. 01

    Idea & market validation

    We assess target customers, market needs and commercial assumptions. The result is a clear business thesis and practical next steps for market validation.

  2. 02

    Business model & business case

    We structure the value proposition, revenue model, cost base and growth path. The business case provides a basis for entrepreneurial decisions and funding discussions.

  3. 03

    Partnerships & company development

    We develop the partnership and governance structure, prepare funding discussions and coordinate company development with the relevant specialists.

  4. 04

    Market entry & execution

    We turn the concept into an operational roadmap and support market entry, focusing on customer outreach, business development and the coordination of the next growth steps.

Outcomes

What you walk away with.

A validated business thesis

Business model & business case

FAQ

Questions about Venture Building

01What is Venture Building?

Venture Building connects business model development with building the company itself: from validating the idea through business case, partnership and governance structure to market entry.

02At what stage does GL Ventures get involved?

From the first business idea through to market entry. Depending on your needs, we support individual stages or the entire build.

03Does GL Ventures support funding?

We develop the business case, set up the partnership and governance structure and prepare funding discussions.
